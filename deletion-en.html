Deleting your MySancho account

App MySancho · developer Artur Baryła · last updated: 22 August 2026

Looking for the wider picture? Privacy policy · Terms of service

This page explains how to delete your MySancho account and exactly what happens to your data afterwards.

Option 1 — in the app

  1. Open MySancho and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profil (Profile) tab.
  3. Choose Usuń konto (Delete account).
  4. Confirm in the dialog.

The account is deleted immediately, with no waiting period. You are signed out automatically and cannot sign back in with the same credentials.

Option 2 — by e-mail

If you no longer have access to the app, write to artbaryl@gmail.com from the e-mail address linked to the account, with "MySancho account deletion" in the subject. We handle such requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

Immediately and permanently:

  • E-mail address — removed from the database.
  • Display name — replaced with an anonymous identifier.
  • Profile picture — erased from file storage.
  • Revolut tag, if one was set.
  • Sign-in account — removed from the authentication system. Signing in stops working straight away, whether you used a password or a Google account.

What is retained

The following stay in the groups you belonged to:

  • expenses, splits and settlements you added,
  • receipt photos you uploaded,
  • your comments.

This content is no longer tied to your identity — it appears under an anonymous identifier, without your name, e-mail address or profile picture.

Why it is not deleted automatically. Expenses and receipts are part of other people's shared settlement. Erasing them would break the balances of the remaining group members, who had no part in your decision — they would lose the record of what they paid for and to whom.

You can request their removal too. Write to artbaryl@gmail.com. We review such requests individually and will tell you how it affects the other members' settlements before acting.

Retention periods

  • Profile data — erased when the account is deleted, with no additional retention period.
  • Server logs (date, time, IP address, response code) — up to 30 days from the event, then deleted automatically. This includes logs recorded before the account was deleted.
  • Group content (expenses, receipts, comments) — kept indefinitely in anonymised form for as long as the group exists. Deleting the group removes them with it.

Just want to stop using the app without losing your data? Instead of deleting the account you can sign out or uninstall the app. Your balances and settlements stay intact and you can come back to them at any time.

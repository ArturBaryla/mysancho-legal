Privacy Policy — MySancho

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Looking for the rules of use? Terms of Service

MySancho is an app for splitting shared costs. This document explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and who can see it.

This is a translation provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Polish version prevails.

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data processed in MySancho is Artur Baryła.

Contact for data matters: artbaryl@gmail.com

2. What data we store

CategorySpecific dataWhere it comes from
Account Email address, first name or display name, profile picture, user identifier From your Google account when you sign in
Groups Group name, description, cover photo, list of members and their roles From you and the other members
Expenses and settlements Amounts, currencies, descriptions, dates, categories, how a cost was split, who paid and who owes whom, recorded repayments From you and the other members of the group

What we do not collect: the app does not read your location, contacts, call history, list of installed apps, or the contents of your gallery. It requests no photo permissions — file selection goes through the Android system picker, which hands the app only the single image you point at. We do not run advertising profiling and we do not sell data.

3. Who has access to your data

The other members of your group

MySancho is a shared app — that is the whole point of it. Everyone who belongs to the same group as you can see:

  • your first name or display name and your profile picture,
  • every expense you have added, along with amounts, descriptions and dates,
  • the receipt photos you have uploaded,
  • your balance — how much you owe and how much is owed to you,
  • your comments and the history of the changes you have made.

Before you upload a photo, consider what else is visible in it.

A receipt photo goes to the server and becomes available to every member of the group — including people who join later. There is no way to "un-show" it to someone who has already seen it.

Do not photograph receipts together with documents, payment cards, account numbers or anything else you would rather not show to the rest of the group.

Photo links are temporary, but work without signing in

Photos are not available at a fixed, public address. The app fetches them through a link generated on demand and valid for 1 hour. During that time the link acts as a key: anyone who receives it can download that photo without signing in. After an hour the link stops working. Do not pass such links on.

The administrator

The app's administrator has technical access to the database and to the photo storage — this is necessary to keep the service running and to fix it. That access is not used to browse user content except where required to resolve a failure or to comply with a legal obligation.

People outside the group

They have access to nothing. Every server function requires authentication, and expense data is visible only within the group you belong to.

4. Who we share data with

We use external providers that process data on our behalf:

ProviderWhat it receivesWhat for
Google (Firebase Authentication) Email address, basic Google profile data Sign-in and identity verification
Google Cloud All application data — accounts, groups, expenses, settlements Server hosting and database
Cloudflare (R2) Receipt photos, group covers, profile pictures Image file storage
Anthropic The receipt photo — only at the moment you use the scanning feature Automatic reading of line items and amounts from the receipt

Receipt scanning sends the photo to Anthropic.

When you use the scanning feature, the receipt photo is sent to the Anthropic service (the Claude model) to recognise the text visible on it. If you do not want the photo to leave our server — do not use scanning, and enter the items manually.

Data may be processed on servers outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers are made on the basis of Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

5. Legal basis and purpose of processing

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — maintaining your account, recording expenses and calculating balances. Without this data the app cannot work.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — server logs, needed to keep the service secure and to diagnose failures.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data and content — until you delete your account.
  • Server logs — up to 30 days.
  • Expenses you added to a group remain visible to the other members even after you delete your account, because they are part of a shared settlement. They are, however, detached from your personal data.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and obtain a copy of it,
  • rectify inaccurate data,
  • erase your data (the "right to be forgotten"),
  • restrict processing,
  • data portability,
  • object to processing.

To exercise any of these rights, write to the address given in section 1. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland).

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account directly in the app, in the profile settings. Deleting the account erases your profile data and the photos you uploaded. You can also request deletion by email at the address in section 1 — we handle such requests within 30 days.

9. Children

The app is not intended for people under 13 years of age and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we learn that such data has reached the app, we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change the scope of the data we collect or the list of providers, we will update this document and change the date at the top of the page. For material changes we will notify you in the app.

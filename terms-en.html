11. Receipt scanning and artificial intelligence

11.1. Scanning is done by an artificial intelligence system: the Claude model by Anthropic, to which the photo is sent, reads the shop, the items and the amounts.

11.2. Check the result of Scanning. The model can make mistakes — misread an amount, miss an item or add one that is not there. Check the data before saving the Expense; you can also correct the items after saving. The reading is a suggestion — you decide what you save in the Group. If Scanning does not work properly (for example, it keeps misreading amounts on clear photos), file a complaint (section 20).

11.3. If you do not want the photo to go to the external provider, do not use Scanning and enter the items by hand.

11.4. Scanning needs an internet connection.

11.5. Scanning that fails for technical reasons — an error on our side, or a reading that does not finish within the time the App waits for it — uses no Tokens.

11.6. If the photo shows no receipt, or the receipt is cut off, covered or unreadable, the first such Scanning within an hour is free and ends without a reading. Each further one within that hour uses Tokens like ordinary Scanning; the App shows what it managed to read.

11.7. The free Scannings in points 11.5 and 11.6 are limited to 5 per hour; beyond that, Scanning is paused for a while. Deliberately causing failed Scannings breaks section 6.

11.8. You add a photo of a receipt at your own responsibility. Before you add it, check what else is visible on it. Do not add photos with sensitive information — about health (for example, a receipt from a pharmacy), card or account numbers, documents or other very personal data: the photo goes to the external Scanning provider (point 11.1), and everyone in the Group sees the Expense with the photo (point 7.1). If you do not want anyone to see it, do not use Scanning and enter the items by hand (point 11.3).

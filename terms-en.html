2. What MySancho is, and what it is not

MySancho is a tool for recording and splitting shared costs. It records who paid for what, divides amounts according to the split you choose, and shows the resulting balances.

MySancho is not a payment service and does not transfer money.

The app does not hold your funds, does not intermediate in payments, and is not a financial institution. The balances shown in the app are information only — a record of what you entered yourselves.

The actual settlement happens outside the app: by bank transfer, in cash, or in any other way you agree between yourselves. Marking a debt as repaid only changes the record in the app and causes no movement of money whatsoever.

If you have provided your Revolut tag, the app can prepare a link that opens Revolut with the amount filled in. That is an ordinary link — you make the payment in Revolut, on Revolut's terms, and MySancho has neither influence over it nor visibility into its outcome.

Settlements between users are their own private matter. MySancho is not a party to them, does not resolve disputes, and does not pursue claims.

Reporting content and users

You can report another user's content — a comment, an expense, a receipt photo, a group's name, description or cover — as well as the user themselves (their name and profile picture) with the "Report" option in the app. You can also report a group and the person who invited you straight from the invitation card, before joining.

  • A report comes to us, not to the author. We do not tell the author who reported their content.
  • Reports are reviewed by a person, without undue delay. Content that breaches this section is removed or replaced with a note that it was removed, and we may take the measures in section 10 against its author.
  • To review a report, we keep a copy of the reported content — for a photo, a copy of the file — even if the author deletes it later. Details in the privacy policy.
  • Knowingly filing false or bulk reports is itself a breach of this section.

Blocking

You can block another user — from that person's menu on the "Members" screen or on an invitation card. A block works on your side only:

  • their comments are hidden for you — you can still reveal them,
  • you do not see their profile picture,
  • their invitations to groups do not reach you.

The blocked person is not told. A block removes no one from any group and does not change shared expenses or balances — those remain part of the group's settlement (section 6). You can lift a block in the account settings, under "Blocked people".

6. Groups and shared settlements

  • An expense added to a group is visible to all its members, including those who join later.
  • Group members can edit shared settlements. The edit history is recorded and visible — it is clear who changed what and when.
  • Expenses you added remain in the group after you leave it or delete your account. This follows from the nature of a shared settlement: removing one item would change everyone else's balances. Such entries are detached from your personal data — details in the privacy policy.

7. Receipt scanning and tickets

The scanning feature reads line items and amounts from a photo of a receipt. Using it requires tickets — the app's internal units of account.

  • A new account receives a starting batch of tickets free of charge.
  • Tickets have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money, and cannot be transferred to another user.
  • Ticket batches have an expiry date — unused tickets are forfeited once it passes.
  • Additional tickets cannot currently be purchased. If we launch sales in the future, the rules on purchase, payment and withdrawal will be described separately, before it goes live.

The scanning result must be checked.

The receipt is read by an artificial intelligence model and it can get things wrong — misread an amount, skip a line item, or add one that is not there. Always verify the recognised data before saving the expense. We are not liable for the consequences of settlements based on an unverified scanning result.

Scanning sends the photo to an external provider (Anthropic). If you do not want that, enter the items manually.
